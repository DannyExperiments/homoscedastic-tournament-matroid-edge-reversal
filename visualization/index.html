How the example proves a matroid difference

The ground set includes every precision coordinate Kij with i ≤ j. In G, K13 and K33 already have a nonzero two-by-two derivative minor. The full triple has rank two because only s and b enter.

In H, the derivative determinant on s, b, c is 2s²c(b² − 1). It is −15/16 at this admissible point where det(I − Λ) = 31/32, so the triple is independent generically. The paper also proves that all twenty triples in H are independent and gives a four-vertex strongly connected construction that lifts to every larger size.

Wu’s dissertation, §5.4.1, p. 63, reports earlier computations that imply small labelled counterexamples. This explorer depicts the explicit three-vertex certificate in the paper.
