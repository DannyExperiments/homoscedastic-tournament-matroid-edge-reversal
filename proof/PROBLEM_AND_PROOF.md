# Statement and full proof

Particular pairs of homoscedastic tournaments related by one edge reversal have different labelled Jacobian matroids in every size from three onward. The pair at size three also has nonisomorphic abstract matroids. At every size from four onward, both tournaments in a labelled counterexample can be strongly connected.

The full proof in [LaTeX](../paper/manuscript.tex) and [PDF](../paper/manuscript.pdf) gives the three-coordinate separator, all-triples argument, strongly connected four-coordinate separator and arbitrary-size lifts. It also preserves the original labelled witness using two transitive tournaments. The [literature audit](../audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md) identifies Wu’s earlier small-case computations and records the overlap.
