Checked November 27, 2026. **High confidence that Wu's dissertation already reports small labelled counterexamples computationally. Moderate confidence that the compact exact polynomial/rank certificates, three-vertex abstract distinction, and uniform strongly connected construction are not covered by the sources inspected.** This search does not establish absolute priority.

## Target and graph convention

The pinned catalog asks about Conjecture 5.4 of Drton, Hollering and Wu. On a fixed labelled vertex set, put one directed edge on every unordered pair. The conjecture says that reversing the edge between the last two labels preserves the Jacobian matroid. Relabelling both graphs simultaneously moves any reversed edge to those labels. The ground set consists of **all** symmetric precision coordinates, including the diagonals. The model is $K=s(I-\Lambda)(I-\Lambda)^{\mathsf T}$, with $s>0$, $I-\Lambda$ invertible, and $\lambda_{ij}$ free on $i\to j$. The support convention follows the displayed model equation, the definition of the edge support, and Lemma 3.1. Propositions 2.9–2.10 in the arXiv text identify the relevant rank criterion and its relationship to generic model distinction. The graphical criteria for unequal outdegrees in Section 4 exclude pairs of complete graphs. ## Direct overlap with Wu's computations

Jun Wu's *Homoscedasticity and Feedback Loops in Graphical Models* was submitted June 28, 2023 and accepted August 28, 2023, as recorded on the title page. The first arXiv version was posted August 3, 2023, between these dates. The paper appeared in the February 2025 journal issue. The dissertation title-page dates record the examination history, while the precise public posting date remains unverified. Section 5.4.1, printed p. 63 (PDF p. 79), reports five labelled matroids among the eight tournaments on three vertices. The two directed cycles have distinct singleton classes and the six transitive triangles fall into three pairs. This already entails a counterexample to Conjecture 5.4 because a single flip of a directed triangle produces a transitive triangle. The four-vertex summary reports twelve equal-matroid pairs, all of them pairs of complete DAGs. It therefore also implies a difference for a pair of strongly connected four-vertex tournaments related by one reversal. The five-vertex summary likewise confines complete equal-matroid pairs to a terminal-vertex reversal. The dissertation explicitly excludes six-vertex tournaments from that computational study on pp. 62–63. [Dissertation §5.4.1, p. 63](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1713538/document.pdf#page=79)

The same dissertation's Theorem 5.28 states generic model distinguishability for a DAG against a graph with a directed cycle. Generic distinguishability alone places weaker constraints than inequality of the coordinate matroids. Corollary 5.22's shared-sink inference requires at least one graph to be non-complete. The case summaries on p. 63 provide the direct evidence for earlier small labelled counterexamples. Peters and Bühlmann's equal-error DAG identifiability theorem deals with full model sets. [Dissertation §5.3](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1713538/document.pdf#page=76) · [Peters and Bühlmann](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/ast043)

## Why the journal's listed check does not cover the full ground set

Conjecture 5.4 is followed by a report of Macaulay2 verification through six vertices. In the linked script `CompleteCyclicSEMs.m2`, line 6 defines a ring including diagonal symbols. That helper does not enter the final comparison. Line 32 returns coordinates indexed by `subsets(n,2)`, line 37 differentiates just that list, and lines 66–71 compare those column matroids after reversal. For a tournament at (Lambda=0, s=1), the off-diagonal Jacobian in the edge variables is a signed permutation matrix, so the restricted matroid is free in either orientation. The result of the listed code addresses that restriction. This observation is about the inspected code path. ## Scope of this release

The release supplies exact three-coordinate certificates and an elementary all-triples argument separating the abstract three-vertex matroids. An independent four-coordinate certificate extends strongly connected labelled counterexamples to every size at least four. Neither the published proposition that different matroids imply generic model distinction nor the dissertation's counts of labelled matroids assert abstract nonisomorphism. The search covered the journal and arXiv versions, the corresponding author's dissertation, the official supplement, its catalog entry, and follow-up queries by the title, author names, conjecture number, tournament edge reversal, counterexample and erratum. No other explicit match for these proof refinements appeared in the sources inspected.
